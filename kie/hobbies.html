Vocabulary / คำศัพท์ – งานอดิเรก

ภาษาไทยPronunciationTranslation

การ + verb / Noun prefix  (p. 3)

Putting การ (gaan) before a verb turns it into a noun — equivalent to English -ing. So tham aa-hǎan ("to cook") becomes gaan tham aa-hǎan ("cooking").

Conversation 1 / บทสนทนา  (p. 9)

Structure 5 / น่า + verb  (p. 10)

Adding น่า (nâa) before a verb makes an adjective meaning worth ___-ing or ___-y / ___-able. So sǒn-jai ("to be interested") becomes nâa sǒn-jai ("interesting / worth interest").
