Use of ดี (dii)

การใช้คำถาม + ดี · expressing deliberation

🎯 Take the quiz of this lesson →
Adding ดี (dii) after a question word transforms a direct question into a softer deliberation — "what shall I…?", "where would be a good place to…?", "who should I…?". It expresses that the speaker is weighing options and seeking advice.
